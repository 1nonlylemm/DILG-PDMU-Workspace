RESEARCH BRIEF · NOVEMBER 2026 MANUSCRIPT

A Centralized Compliance Monitoring, Evaluation, and Tracking System for LGU Requirement Submissions in DILG Laguna PDMU

Luis Mario F. Acovera · Ma. Lena Therese S. Quizon · Mark Andrew Z. Soliman
BS Information Technology · College of Computer Studies
Laguna State Polytechnic University, Santa Cruz

Thesis adviser: Reynalen C. Justo, LPT, DIT

Context and objective

The study addresses fragmented documentary compliance monitoring through separate Excel files and Google Drive folders in DILG-PDMU Laguna. It aims to design, develop and evaluate an internal web platform that consolidates monitoring across the 30 LGUs in the province.

System scope

COMET covers compliance monitoring, document intake and review, document uploading and versioning, deadline and notice management, workbook import and mapping, analytics and reporting, archival and communication history, audit trails, OCR/NLP support, a smart assistant and Google Drive regional handoff.

Methodology

The research uses a descriptive developmental design and Agile development. Its evaluation includes functional testing of core modules and user acceptance evaluation among 36 participants.

Results reported in the manuscript

  • 147 functional test steps across 13 core modules, with a reported 100% success rate.
  • Acceptance grand mean: 4.30 out of 5, interpreted as Highly Acceptable.
  • Quality Factors: 4.31; Perceived Ease of Use: 4.33; Attitude Towards Using: 4.38; Behavioral Intention to Use: 4.31. Each is interpreted as Highly Acceptable.
  • User Experience: 4.19, interpreted as Acceptable.

These are study-specific results reported in the supplied manuscript. They do not establish a guarantee of system uptime, security, general model accuracy or future user outcomes.

Technology

The documented system uses React on the frontend and Flask, SQLAlchemy, Alembic, JWT and Celery on the backend, with PostgreSQL, Redis and document-processing/integration services.
